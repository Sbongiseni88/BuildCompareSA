# 🎬 BuildCompare SA — Demo Video Guide

## Option A: Auto-Play Showcase (EASIEST — No Login Needed)

This is a self-contained presentation page that auto-advances through feature slides with smooth animations. Perfect for a polished LinkedIn video.

### How to Record

1. **Open in Chrome**: Go to `http://localhost:3000/demo-showcase.html`
2. **Full Screen**: Press `F11` for distraction-free recording
3. **Start Recording**: Press `Win + G` → click the record button (or use OBS Studio)
4. **Let it play**: The slides auto-advance every 5-7 seconds
5. **Stop Recording** after the final CTA slide

**Keyboard Controls:**
- `→` / `Space` — Next slide
- `←` — Previous slide
- Click the progress dots at the bottom to jump

**Recommended screen size**: 1280×720 or 1920×1080

## 🎵 Post-Production Tips

1. **Add background music**: Use royalty-free music (e.g., from YouTube Audio Library)
2. **Trim to 60s**: LinkedIn favors short, punchy videos
3. **Add captions**: 85% of LinkedIn videos are watched on mute
4. **Upload natively**: Don't share a YouTube link — upload directly to LinkedIn
5. **Post timing**: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM SAST for best engagement

## 📐 Video Specs for LinkedIn
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (landscape) or 1:1 (square — better engagement)
- **Resolution**: 1080p minimum
- **Length**: 30-90 seconds ideal
- **Format**: MP4
- **Max file size**: 5GB
